# RoB 2: a revised tool for assessing risk of bias in randomised trials

- **Penulis:** Jonathan A C Sterne, Jelena Savović, Matthew J. Page, Roy G. Elbers
- **Jurnal:** BMJ
- **Tahun:** 2019 | **Disitasi:** 32781x
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l4898
- **Konsep:** Systematic review, Medicine, Randomized controlled trial, Publication bias

## Abstrak
Assessment of risk of bias is regarded as an essential component of a systematic review on the effects of an intervention. The most commonly used tool for randomised trials is the Cochrane risk-of-bias tool. We updated the tool to respond to developments in understanding how bias arises in randomised trials, and to address user feedback on and limitations of the original tool.
