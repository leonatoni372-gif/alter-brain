# Current and future role of Haber–Bosch ammonia in a carbon-free energy landscape

- **Penulis:** Collin Smith, Alfred K. Hill, Laura Torrente‐Murciano
- **Jurnal:** Energy & Environmental Science
- **Tahun:** 2019 | **Disitasi:** 1972x
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1039/c9ee02873k
- **Konsep:** Renewable energy, Ammonia, Environmental science, Methane
- **Core:** [[Core Ilmu]]

## Abstrak
The future of green ammonia as long-term energy storage relies on the replacement of the conventional CO 2 intensive methane-fed Haber–Bosch process by distributed and agile ones aligned to the geographically isolated and intermittent renewable energy.
