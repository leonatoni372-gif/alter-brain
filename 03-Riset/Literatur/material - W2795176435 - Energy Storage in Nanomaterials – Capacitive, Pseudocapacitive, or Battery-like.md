## Abstrak
There are increasing numbers of new electrode materials (e.g., transitionmetal oxides, hydroxides, sulfides, carbides, nitrides, conducting polymers, etc.) that display electrochemical characteristics that are neither purely capacitive nor purely Faradaic.
